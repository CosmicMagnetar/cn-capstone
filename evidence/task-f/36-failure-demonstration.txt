Option A — Stop one backend.

Before:
Backend A (10.7.22.10:3001) and Backend B (10.7.7.25:3002) were running. Requests through the nginx edge returned both X-Backend: A and X-Backend: B.

Action:
Backend A was stopped on Mac 3.

After:
Requests continued successfully through Backend B and returned X-Backend: B only.

Affected layer:
The backend/application layer was affected because Backend A on TCP port 3001 was unavailable. DNS and the nginx edge remained running.

Restoration:
Backend A was started again on Mac 3. After restoration, requests through the nginx edge again returned both X-Backend: A and X-Backend: B.
